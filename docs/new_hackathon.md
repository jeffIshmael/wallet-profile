Build agentic payment applications and DeFAI products on **Celo**, an Ethereum L2 built for fast, low-cost global payments, leveraging the robust agentic tooling and infrastructure for bounties from a **$5K prize pool, distributed in CELO.**

Celo's tooling and ecosystem enable AI agents to deploy across global payments, mobile-first applications, and stablecoin-native transactions, with 16M+ MiniPay users ready to put them to work. Teams are expected to go beyond prototypes; winning agents generate real transactions and demonstrate genuine utility onchain. If you're interested in agents with real economic agency and global distribution, this is the place to build.

## 🏆 **Prize Distribution**

### Track 1: **Most Revenue Generated - $3,000 (Distributed in $CELO)**

🥇 1st Prize: $2000
🥈 2nd Prize: $1000 

- **What you need to know about this track:**
    - **Win condition:** the agent that generates the most on-chain volume on Celo during the hackathon (Jul 7–20).
    - **How to submit/get tracked:** integrate **Attribution Tags** (you will get the code when registering for the hackathon) so your agent's transactions are tagged on-chain, and **submit a link** to your agent's on-chain wallet address/ tagged transactions.
    - **Ideas:** build any revenue-generating agent, like a DeFi product or an FX trading agent.

### Track 2: **Most x402 Payments - $1,000 (Distributed in $CELO)**

🥇 1st Prize: $700 
🥈 2nd Prize: $300 

- **What you need to know about this track:**
    - **Win condition:** the agent or app that settles the **most x402 payments on Celo** during the hackathon — raw count of successful pay-per-request transactions.
    - **How to submit/get tracked:** route payments through the Celo x402 facilitator and register your payTo wallet + endpoint; on-chain x402 transactions to that address are counted via the dashboard.
    - **Prize & eligibility:** Open to anything built during the hackathon (Jul 7–20) settling real x402 payments on Celo; transactions will only be counted during the hackathon period.

### Track 3: **Askbots - $500 (Distributed in CELO)**

🥇 Total in Bounties: $500

- **What you need to know about this track:**
    - **Win condition:** Judging and leaderboard bounties for the highest-rated agents on askbots.ai.
    - **How to submit/get tracked:** Register your judge agent on AskBot. Submit your project through Celo Builder Skill.

### Track 4: Best Feedback for Aigora **- $500 (Distributed in CELO)**

🥇 Total in Bounties: $500

- **What you need to know about this track:**
    - **Win condition:** The top 10 most valuable feedback submissions each win $50 in CELO.
    - **How to submit/get tracked:** Register your agent on aigora.org/, submit your feedback, and submit your agentic project through the Celo Builder Skill.

## ❓FAQ

- **How are the winners for each track determined?**
    - The winner will be selected according to the focus of each track considering a combination of project’s alignment with the ecosystem mission, delivery of consistent transactions and onchain activity, and real-world utility.
    - Our incredible judges will conduct an additional manual review to ensure fair judgment and identify any attempts at sybil attacks.
- **What are Attribution Tags?**
    
    Attribution Codes are built on ERC-8021 and shipped as a free, open-source SDK (`@celo/attribution-tags` on npm), added with one line of code. It doesn't change what a transaction does, it just tags it as coming from your project so it's visible on a public dashboard (available soon). Learn more here.
    
- **What is x402?**
    
    Celo’s x402 facilitator is HTTP 402-based instant stablecoin micropayments for agents/APIs.
    
- **Do I have to use OpenClaw, or can I use any agent framework?**
    
    You can use any agent framework.
