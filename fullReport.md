# The full report of a wallet (the heart)

- This is an official pdf that contains the financial attestation of a wallet.
- Its more transparent than the normal pdf.
- It contains the following:-

## 1. Identity
    - wallet address
    - connected ENS (if any)
    - active chains
    - wallet age
    - report ID
    - generated timestamp

## 2. Financial Summary
    - total inflow
    - total outflow
    - average monthly inflow
    - stablecoin holdings
    - active protocols
    - portfolio value

## 2.1 3-Month Cash Flow Statement (Lipa Mdogo Mdogo Compliance)
    - 3-Month Total Inflow (USD/KES)
    - 3-Month Total Outflow (USD/KES)
    - Net Cash Flow (Inflow - Outflow)
    - Timeframe Transaction Count
    - Micro-repayment status indicator (Highly Eligible, Eligible, Needs Review)

## 3. Financial Health
- This is the hero section.
- Include:- (And explain WHY.)
    - Financial Health Score
    - Reputation Score
    - Risk Score
    - Stability Score

## 4. Income Analysis
- VERY important for Saccos.
- Include:
    - recurring inflows
    - estimated monthly earnings
    - consistency analysis
    - largest inflow sources
Example:
“Wallet demonstrates recurring weekly inflows over 9 months.”
That’s powerful.

## 5. Loan Suitability
- Based on:
    - inflow consistency,
    - retained balances,
    - stablecoin reserves,
    - risk exposure.
- This makes the report PRACTICAL.
Example:
Estimated Safe Borrowing Capacity
250 – 800 USD

## 6. Risk Assessment
Include:
    - volatility exposure
    - suspicious drains
    - unstable trading behavior
    - liquidation exposure

## 7. AI Financial Summary
Example:
“This wallet shows moderate financial discipline with consistent stablecoin inflows and healthy balance retention. The wallet demonstrates low speculative exposure and strong repayment potential.”
That sounds AMAZING in demos.

## 8. Verification & Attestation
Include:
    - report hash
    - signed attestation
    - ERC-804 agent identity
    - verification endpoint
Just to make it feel institutional.
