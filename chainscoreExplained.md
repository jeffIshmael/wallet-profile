# ChainScore

## AI-Powered Financial Reputation for the Onchain Economy

### Overview

ChainScore is an AI-powered financial reputation platform that transforms onchain wallet activity into verifiable proof of income, financial reputation, and lending insights.

Millions of freelancers, creators, remote workers, and digital entrepreneurs receive payments in cryptocurrency. While these individuals may have a strong and consistent income stream, they often face a major challenge when applying for loans, mortgages, or financial products:

**They cannot easily prove their income.**

Traditional financial institutions rely on bank statements, payslips, employment contracts, and credit histories to evaluate borrowers. Crypto-native users often lack these documents despite having substantial and consistent onchain earnings.

ChainScore bridges this gap.

By analyzing wallet activity, transaction history, portfolio behavior, and financial consistency, ChainScore generates a comprehensive financial reputation profile that users can share with lenders, Saccos, microfinance institutions, DAOs, and other financial service providers.

---

# The Problem

## Proof of Income is Broken for Crypto Users

A growing number of people earn their income through:

* Freelancing
* Remote work
* DAO contributions
* Stablecoin payments
* Onchain businesses
* Creator economies
* DeFi activities

When these users seek credit, lenders typically ask for:

* Payslips
* Bank statements
* Employment records
* Credit history

Crypto wallets contain valuable financial data, but that information is fragmented, difficult to interpret, and not presented in a format that lenders can understand.

As a result:

* Freelancers struggle to qualify for loans.
* Crypto earners appear financially invisible.
* Lenders cannot accurately assess risk.
* Valuable financial history remains locked inside wallets.

# Our Solution

ChainScore converts onchain activity into a financial reputation layer.

Users connect their wallet and receive an AI-powered analysis of their financial behavior.

The platform analyzes:

* Wallet age
* Transaction history
* Income consistency
* Stablecoin holdings
* Savings behavior
* Portfolio composition
* Financial health indicators
* Reputation signals
* Risk exposure

Using these signals, ChainScore generates a financial profile that can be understood by both crypto-native users and traditional financial institutions.

# How It Works

### Step 1: Connect Wallet

Users connect their wallet to ChainScore.

The current MVP focuses on the Celo ecosystem, with future support planned for additional EVM-compatible chains.

---

### Step 2: Analyze Financial Activity

ChainScore evaluates wallet behavior and calculates:

* Financial Health Score
* Reputation Score
* Income Stability Score
* Savings Behavior
* Risk Profile
* Portfolio Insights

The platform identifies patterns that may indicate reliable income streams and responsible financial management.

---

### Step 3: Generate Lending Insights

The platform estimates:

* Monthly income trends
* Financial stability
* Savings consistency
* Recommended borrowing capacity

This helps lenders and borrowers better understand a wallet's ability to support credit.

---

### Step 4: Download a Verified Financial Report

Users can purchase a premium Financial Reputation Report for 0.1 USDT.

The report includes:

* Wallet identity summary
* Financial Health Score
* Reputation Score
* Income analysis
* Cash flow analysis
* Savings analysis
* Risk assessment
* Estimated loan capacity
* AI-generated lending assessment

The report is designed to serve as a professional proof-of-income document for lenders and financial institutions.

# The OnFRA Agent

ChainScore is powered by OnFRA.

**OnFRA (Onchain Financial Reputation Agent)** is an ERC-8004 agent responsible for analyzing wallet activity and generating financial reputation assessments.

OnFRA acts as the intelligence layer behind the platform by:

* Evaluating financial behavior
* Calculating reputation metrics
* Assessing lending suitability
* Producing financial attestations
* Generating lender-ready reports
