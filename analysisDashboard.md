# The analysis Dashoard
- This is the dashboard shown to the user after we have analysed their connected wallet.
- The most important details to show are:-

## 1. Financial Health (%) 
- Explain why
- What Contributes To It:-

### Income Stability
 - consistent inflows?
 - random inflows?
 - salary-like behavior?
### Savings Discipline
 - keeps balances?
 - instantly drains wallet?
 - stablecoin retention?
### Portfolio Risk
 - all meme coins?
 - diversified?
 - mostly stablecoins?
### Spending Discipline
 - excessive outflows?
 - controlled spending?
 - frequent drains?
### Wallet Maturity
 - wallet age
 - protocol interactions
 - trusted ecosystem activity
### Debt / Risk Signals
 - liquidation history
 - suspicious patterns
 - excessive leverage

The explanation should be like 
 - e.g “Your wallet shows strong income consistency and healthy stablecoin retention, but high volatility exposure reduces your overall score.”

## 2. Income stability meter & Cash Flow Statement
- It should be fun labels.
- The user can select the transaction timeframe (last 1 month, last 3 months, last 6 months, last 1 year) to view the statement.
### Metrics
 - weekly inflow consistency
 - monthly income estimate
 - average inflow size
 - recurring sender patterns
 - custom timeframe cash flow statement:
   - Timeframe Total Inflow (USD/KES)
   - Timeframe Total Outflow (USD/KES)
   - Net Cash Flow (Inflow - Outflow)
   - Transaction Count in timeframe

### The fun labels
 - Stable Earner
 - Growing Wallet
 - Seasonal Earner
 - Volatile Income
 - Whale Activity
 - Dormant Wallet

## 3. Wallet Reputation Score (%)
- Separate from financial health.
- This measures trustworthiness.
### Signals
 - wallet age
 - scam interactions
 - suspicious drains
 - transaction consistency
 - repayment patterns
 - protocol credibility

Example Output
Reputation Score: 91/100
“This wallet demonstrates long-term legitimate activity across trusted protocols.”
