# ChainScore Landing Page V2 Update

## Objective

The current sections feel like a generic SaaS product.

The goal is to transform the landing page into a story about solving the proof-of-income problem for crypto earners.

Visitors should understand:

1. Why lenders struggle to evaluate crypto income.
2. How ChainScore converts wallet activity into financial trust.
3. What they receive after analysis.
4. Why the report is valuable.
5. Why they should pay 0.1 USDT.

# Overall Design Direction

The landing page should feel closer to:

* Stripe
* Mercury
* Ramp
* Arc

and less like:

* Generic crypto analytics dashboard

The emotional message should be:

"Your wallet already proves your income. ChainScore makes it understandable."
